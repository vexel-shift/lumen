Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Lumen is designed to be easy to edit. The structure is plain and well-organized, so you can change text and colors without any programming background.

Can I use this for a commercial project?

Yes. Lumen is free to use for personal and commercial projects alike. There are no licensing fees or hidden costs.

Does it work on mobile?

Absolutely. Every section adapts to the screen it's viewed on, from small phones to large desktop monitors..

How do I get support?

Use the contact form below or email us directly. Pro and Team plans include priority support with faster response times..

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time. No long-term commitments.
